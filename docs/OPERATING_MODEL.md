# Operating Model & RACI Guidance

| Activity | Accountable | Responsible | Consulted |
|---|---|---|---|
| Technology strategy | CIO | IT strategy office | CTO, CISO, business leaders |
| Product/engineering architecture | CTO | Architecture & engineering | CIO, CISO, product owners |
| Security policy and cyber risk | CISO | Security team | CIO, CTO, Legal, Risk |
| Enterprise architecture | CIO / designated EA sponsor | Enterprise Architect | CTO, business owners, CISO |
| Security architecture | CISO | Security Architect | CTO, enterprise architect |
| IT control operation | Relevant service owner | Control owner | CISO, GRC |
| Independent audit | Audit Committee / CAE | Internal Audit | Management, risk owners |
| Risk acceptance | Authorized business risk owner | Risk owner | CISO, Legal, GRC |
| Compliance obligation interpretation | Compliance / Legal lead | Compliance team | Business and control owners |
| System hardening | Infrastructure / platform owner | Systems engineering | Security operations, CISO |

RACI must be tailored to the organization's size, regulatory context and delegated authority. Internal Audit must remain independent from management control ownership.
