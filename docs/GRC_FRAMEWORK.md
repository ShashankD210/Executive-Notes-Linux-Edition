# Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC) Framework

## Governance
Define board/committee oversight, executive accountability, delegated authorities, policy hierarchy, risk appetite, exception approval and reporting cadence. Maintain a RACI for each control family.

## Risk management process
Context → identify → analyze → evaluate → treat → monitor → communicate. Maintain risk ID, asset/process, threat, vulnerability, cause, impact, likelihood, inherent rating, existing controls, residual rating, treatment, owner, due date and acceptance authority.

## Control management
Each control has a stable ID, objective, risk linkage, owner role, implementation guidance, evidence expectations, frequency, framework mappings, design/operating assessment and exception state. Distinguish control existence from operating effectiveness.

## Policy lifecycle
Draft → owner review → security/privacy/legal review → approval → publication/communication → attestation/training → periodic review → retirement. Record version, approver and effective date.

## Third-party risk
Tier suppliers by data sensitivity, criticality, access, concentration and substitutability. Perform due diligence, contract/security clauses, ongoing monitoring, incident notification review, exit planning and evidence-based reassessment.

## Risk acceptance
Time-bound, scoped and documented; include business rationale, compensating controls, residual exposure, expiry date and authorized approver. High/critical residual risks require escalation per risk appetite.

## Reporting
Report exposure and trend, not only checklist completion: critical risks, overdue high findings, control failures, exceptions nearing expiry, supplier concentration, audit issues, resilience tests and regulatory deadlines.
