# Security & Compliance Management

## Compliance operating cycle
1. Identify applicable jurisdictions, sectors, contracts and regulator expectations.
2. Maintain an obligations register with source, applicability rationale, accountable owner, effective date, review date and evidence.
3. Map obligations to policies, risks, controls, systems and suppliers.
4. Assess design and operating effectiveness; document gaps and compensating measures.
5. Track remediation, notifications, attestations and periodic reviews.
6. Retain records under approved schedules and legal holds.

## Reference frameworks (applicability must be assessed)
- ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and 27002:2022 — ISMS and information security controls.
- NIST CSF 2.0; NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 — cybersecurity outcomes and control catalog.
- CIS Controls v8.1 and CIS Benchmarks — prioritized safeguards and configuration baselines.
- COBIT 2019 — governance and management objectives.
- ISO 22301 — business continuity management.
- OWASP ASVS / API Security Top 10 — application and API assurance.
- GDPR and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 — only where applicable; verify current rules, commencement, exemptions and sectoral overlays with counsel.

## Important distinction
A framework crosswalk is not a legal applicability decision, certification, audit opinion or guarantee of compliance. Keep authoritative obligation text and legal interpretations under controlled ownership. Record exact version and source date for every mapping.

## Privacy controls
Data inventory, purpose limitation, minimization, lawful basis/notice where required, data subject rights workflows where applicable, processor/vendor controls, retention/deletion, breach assessment and notification decision process.
